# Feature maturity

Not every capability in CHILI GraFx is at the same stage. This page explains the labels you will see on release notes and documentation pages, so you know what you can rely on and what may still change.

## Alpha

A proof of concept, built to validate an idea. It exists to learn from real use before we commit to building the capability properly.

Alphas are meant for exploration and feedback, not for work on production design systems. They often live in a separate workspace, can change or be withdrawn without notice, and are not covered by service level objectives (SLOs). Available for CHILI employees, and enabled for a client environment on request.

## Beta

The capability is integrated in the product and carries a **beta** label. It works with your real data and is safe for day-to-day work, but it is still moving: interfaces and behavior can change between releases, and feedback from beta users shapes what gets built before general availability. Available for CHILI employees, and enabled for a client environment on request.

For developers: treat beta endpoints as unstable and re-test your integration after each release.

## Production

The default. No label, available to every client, and covered by the normal release process — announced in [release notes](/release-notes/), documented, and subject to deprecation notices before anything is removed. AI-driven capabilities remain subject to the environment-level AI opt-out.

## GraFx Labs

[GraFx Labs](/CHILI-GraFx/concepts/grafx-labs/) is a dedicated area inside CHILI GraFx that hosts early, often AI-driven capabilities. It is a **location**, not a maturity stage — the stages above still describe how finished something in Labs is.

Capabilities there can change or be removed at any time and are not covered by SLOs. Each render is charged as a production output, so GraFx Labs is not available on [Sandbox](/CHILI-GraFx/concepts/sandbox/).

## Experimental (being retired)

**Experimental** was the single label for anything released early — features, API endpoints and plugins alike. It is being replaced by Alpha and Beta, which say more precisely how finished something is and who can get access to it. New capabilities are no longer labelled Experimental.

You may still see the label on a few pages while the change is rolled out. Historical release notes keep the wording they were published with.

## Getting access

Alpha and Beta capabilities are enabled per environment on request. Contact your account manager or [our support team](/CHILI-GraFx/support/).

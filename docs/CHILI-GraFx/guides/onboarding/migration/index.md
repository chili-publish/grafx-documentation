# User Account Migration

## 1. Your setup is ready

You have received an email, notifying your account is setup.

![Account-created email from the CHILI Customer Success Team, with the password reset link arrowed](migrate-1.png){.steps}

## 2. Reset your password

### Start reset password

Before you start, reset your password. [here](https://my.chili-publish.com/Account/ResetPassword), using your email as your login.

![Reset password page with the Username field filled in and the red Reset password button arrowed](migrate-2.png){.steps}

### Confirm the email

You will receive an email, where you need to follow the link to confirm you initiated the password reset.

![Password change email pointing at the "here" link you click to confirm the reset request](migrate-3.png){.steps}

### Enter new password

The link in the email will bring you to a page to enter your new password.

Your password should follow these guidelines:

- Contain at least 16 characters
- Cannot contain parts of your full name that exceed 2 consecutive characters
- Must contain characters from 3 of the following 4 categories
	- uppercase characters (A - Z)
	- lowercase characters (a - z)
	- base 10 digits (0 - 9)
	- non-alphabetic characters (eg, !, $, #, %)

Confirm "Change password"

![New password and Confirm new password fields both filled, with Change password arrowed](migrate-4.png){.steps}

## 3. Logging in

### You have not migrated yet

Go to [My CHILI publish](https://my.chili-publish.com) and log in.

![The same Change password form, completed, before you return to My CHILI publish to log in](migrate-5.png){.steps}

!!! Warning
	You did migrate before?

	Go to [CHILI GraFx](https://chiligrafx.com/) to log in

## 4. Migration to CHILI GraFx

These steps are only necessary, if you did not migrate yet

### Migration prompt

The first time you arrive on CHILI GraFx, arriving from My CHILI publish, you'll be prompted to migrate.

![Migration prompt "We're moving your account to our new platform!" with the Let's do this! button arrowed](migrate-6.png){.steps}

### Confirm your email address

The Email field will be pre-populated with your login (email).
Confirm your email.

![Enter your email step with the Email field pre-populated and Confirm your email arrowed](migrate-7.png){.steps}

### E-mail link

You'll receive an email, where you need to confirm the migration to CHILI GraFx.

![Confirm your email step with an envelope illustration and a Resend email link](migrate-8.png){.steps}

![Migration email "Please confirm your email and set a password", with the reset-password link arrowed](migrate-9.png){.steps}

### Confirm your new password

On the page in CHILI GraFx, confirm your new password.

![CHILI GraFx New password screen with every password requirement ticked green above Update password](migrate-10.png){.steps}

![Password updated confirmation, a tick inside a circle above the success message](migrate-11.png){.steps}

## 5. Logging in to CHILI GraFx

Go to [CHILI GraFx](https://chiligrafx.com/) to login with your credentials

![CHILI GraFx Welcome screen asking only for an email, with the Login button arrowed](migrate-12.png){.steps}

![Second Welcome step: the email now shown with an Edit link, Password filled, Continue arrowed](migrate-13.png){.steps}

Congratulations, you are now ready to start your **Creative Automation** journey!

## Where to go now?

### Support platform

In the platform, you can click the question mark, in the bottom-right corner.
Click "Support" to access the support platform. (use the same CHILI GraFx credentials to login).

![The round dark blue question mark help button from the bottom-right corner of the platform](migrate-14.png){.steps}

![Help menu listing Documentation, Support, Release notes and Get certified, with Support highlighted](migrate-15.png){.steps}

![Support portal login with Remember me ticked and the Log in with GraFx button arrowed](migrate-16.png){.steps}

Choose the "Log in with GraFx" button.

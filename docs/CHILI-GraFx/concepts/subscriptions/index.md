# CHILI GraFx Subscriptions

Your account can hold 1 or more subscriptions. Choosing "My subscriptions" in the account drop down will display a dashboard of the first active subscription.

![Summary dashboard of the first active subscription](https://chilipublishdocs.imgix.net/CHILI_GraFx/mysubscription_summary.png){.screenshot-full}

![Add-ons section of the same subscription dashboard](https://chilipublishdocs.imgix.net/CHILI_GraFx/mysubscription_addons.png){.screenshot-full}

The subscription details show the **Subscription ID**, which you can copy, for example when you contact support or work with the Platform API.

If you have more than 1 subscription, you can change subscriptions in the dropdown next to the home button.

![Subscription dropdown next to the home button, used to switch between subscriptions](https://chilipublishdocs.imgix.net/CHILI_GraFx/myaccount4.png?w=830&q=80){.screenshotsmall}

# Add-ons

## Template designer seats

For a designer to be able to make a template, you need to assign a Template Designer Seat to this user. These seats can be re-assigned to other users over time.

## Renders

Renders is a measure of usage of the platform. See [**renders**](/CHILI-GraFx/concepts/renders/) for more detail.

This number shows the rolling average of renders over the period in your contract: 3 months or 6 months. The label shows which period applies. This is also the basis for the invoicing, see [how usage is calculated](/CHILI-GraFx/concepts/renders/#how-do-you-calculate-my-usage).

## Storage

Shows the amount of storage your environment or subscription uses.

## Sandbox environments

Shows the amount of sandbox environments your subscription uses.

## Production environments

Shows the amount of production environments your subscription uses.

## Staging tenants

Shows the amount of staging tenants your subscription uses.
